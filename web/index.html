PUBLISHED RESEARCH

Deep Learning Based Brain Tumor Classification Using Orange Data Analytics

Deep Learning Based Brain Tumor Classification Using Orange Data Analytics

IEEE SCEECS 2026 DOI: 10.1109/SCEECS68810.2026.11429884 March 2026 6 Authors · 2 Institutions

This research presents a brain tumor classification workflow using the Orange image data mining tool. The pipeline includes InceptionV3 image embeddings, dimensionality reduction with PCA, and systematic evaluation of six ML algorithms using k-fold (k=3, 5, 10) cross-validation. The neural network model consistently achieves classification accuracy above 96% and AUC up to 0.997, reaching a peak of 99.0% accuracy with AUC 0.999 in 10-fold validation — a +7% improvement over prior work.

Methodology
01 — DATA COLLECTION

16K+ MRI Images

02 — PREPROCESSING

Augmentation

Resize to 224×224, normalization. Augmentation: ±15° rotation, horizontal flip, ±20% brightness/contrast, Gaussian noise σ=0.1.

+8% MCC gain
03 — FEATURE EXTRACTION

InceptionV3 CNN

InceptionV3 CNN → 2048-dim vectors per scan. Combined with handcrafted texture, contrast, and mean intensity features.

2048-dim vectors
04 — PCA REDUCTION

Dimensionality Cut

Reduces 2048-dim to 100–150 components retaining 95% of variance. Eliminates redundancy and reduces computation time by 70%.

−70% compute
05 — MODEL TRAINING

6 Classifiers

Neural Network (Adam), SVM, Random Forest (n=100), Logistic Regression, Naive Bayes, Decision Tree — all evaluated under k-fold CV.

OUR MISSION

Brain tumors affect millions globally. Early detection dramatically improves patient outcomes. Yet most AI research stays locked in academic papers — inaccessible to the clinicians and patients who could benefit most.

We took our IEEE-published research and built it into a tool anyone with an MRI image can use. No medical imaging software required. No technical knowledge needed. Just upload and analyze.

We are Computational Intelligence students at SRM Institute of Science and Technology, Kattankulathur, and Computer Science students at NIT Puducherry. This started as a research paper and became something we're genuinely proud of.
